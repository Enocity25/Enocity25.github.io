Brand kit — v2 — November 2026

One mark, one typeface, one accent.

Everything here is built from the Enoch A logo: its black, its grey, its shape, and the Outfit typeface. Version 2 is quieter and more cinematic: flat surfaces, sharp corners, real film frames, and no glossy 3D effects. Use this page for the website, client quotes, proposals, thumbnails and social posts so they all look like one studio.

02 Colour

Two colours from the logo, one accent

Logo Black and Logo Grey do most of the work, alternating section by section like scenes in a film. Studio Green is the only accent: it marks the thing you want people to do or notice. The colour in the work itself comes from the films.

03 Typography

Outfit, with a mono for labels

Outfit carries the brand: headlines, body text and the wordmark. Its geometric shapes match the straight cuts of the E. JetBrains Mono is used small, in capitals, for labels, numbers and running times, the way a film slate or a credit sheet would. Both are free on Google Fonts (Outfit, JetBrains Mono).

04 — Graphic device

The window

The E is used as a window onto the films: a frame from the work shows through the exact shape of the mark. The logo stays untouched, and it carries real footage instead of effects. Use it once per layout, large.

The window. A film frame inside the mark. Website hero, reel covers, profile banners.

The sign-off. The full lockup set wide across the width. Website footer, end cards of films, the last page of a proposal.

Texture

A very light film grain (about 7%) sits over the website so flat colour doesn't look digital. Corners are square or 2 px. Colour and motion come from three places: soft Studio Green light in dark sections, one Green Deep section (the process), and moving green ribbons listing the styles. No glossy gradients or glass effects.
