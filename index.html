How a film gets made

You bring the story. I handle everything between the idea and the final export.

01

Script and shot list

We agree the story, the length and the look. I break it into scenes and shots before anything is generated.

02

Characters and sets

Each character gets a reference sheet. Each location gets a plate. You approve them first, so nothing drifts later.

03

Generation

Shots are generated with Google Veo, Seedance, Nano Banana and Higgsfield, with voice from ElevenLabs, and checked for continuity.

04

Edit and delivery

Music, sound, titles and colour in CapCut or After Effects. You get 16:9 and 9:16 exports, with one round of revisions included.
