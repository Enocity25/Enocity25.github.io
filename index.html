How a film gets made

You bring the story. I handle everything between the idea and the final export.

Script and shot list

We agree the story, the length and the look. I break it into scenes and shots before anything is generated.

Characters and sets

Each character gets a reference sheet. Each location gets a plate. You approve them first, so nothing drifts later.

Generation

Shots are generated with Google Veo, Seedance and Nano Banana, with voice from ElevenLabs, and checked for continuity.

Edit and delivery

Music, sound, titles and colour in CapCut or After Effects. You get 16:9 and 9:16 exports, with one round of revisions included.

Hi, I'm Enoch.

I'm an AI video creator making Pixar-style 3D animated films for clients who have a story to tell but no animation studio to tell it.

I work from a script, a storyboard or just an idea, and take it through character design, scene planning, generation and editing until it's a finished film. Recent work includes a 60-second story ad for a design agency, a Dutch-language business explainer, a 3D-animated TV horror episode, a couple's wedding story, and educational series for children and for workplaces.

Every character is locked with reference sheets before a single shot is generated, so faces, outfits and sets stay consistent from the first scene to the last. I also build n8n and Google Apps Script automations for teams that want their spreadsheets, email and documents to run themselves.
