03 — Process

How a film gets made

You bring the story. I handle everything between the idea and the final export.

Step 01

Script and shot list

We agree the story, the length and the look. I break it into scenes and shots before anything is generated.

Step 02

Characters and sets

Each character gets a reference sheet and each location a plate. You approve them first, so nothing drifts later.

Step 03

Generation

Shots are generated with Google Veo, Seedance, Nano Banana and Higgsfield, with voice from ElevenLabs, and checked for continuity.

Step 04

Edit and delivery

Music, sound, titles and colour in CapCut or After Effects. 16:9 and 9:16 exports, with one round of revisions included.

06 — About

I'm Enoch. I make AI animated films for people who have a story to tell but no animation studio to tell it.

I work from a script, a storyboard or just an idea, and take it through character design, scene planning, generation and editing until it's a finished film. Recent work includes story ads for a web design agency, a dental clinic and a car detailing clinic, a Dutch-language business explainer, a couple's wedding story and episodes for an animated series.

I work across styles: Pixar-style 3D, realistic and cinematic, 2D cartoon, anime, claymation and other stylised looks. We pick the one that suits your story and audience before anything is made.

Every character is locked with reference sheets before a single shot is generated, so faces, outfits and sets stay consistent from the first scene to the last.

Tools — Google Veo 3.1 / Google Flow / Nano Banana Pro / Seedance 2.0 / Gemini Omni Flash / Higgsfield / Blender / ElevenLabs / CapCut / After Effects
